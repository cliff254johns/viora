## 2. What personal data do we process?

Depending on how you interact with us, we may process:

- **Identity and business contact data:** name, job title, business email, business telephone, employer, business address and preferred contact channel.
- **Business and enquiry data:** organisation name, website, sector, business size where provided, requested services, project requirements and communications.
- **Account and service data:** account identifiers, authentication and role information, settings, support requests, service usage and security events.
- **Commercial records:** proposals, orders, invoices, payment status and transaction references. Payment card details are handled by the relevant payment provider where applicable; confirm actual flows before publication.
- **Marketing preference and compliance data:** consent wording/version, date and method of consent, source and collection date, privacy notice version, notice delivery evidence, legitimate-interest assessment reference, objections, unsubscribe/suppression status, campaign/message reference and approval history.
- **Technical and usage data:** IP address, device/browser information, access logs, pages and features used, error/security events and approximate location derived from IP address, subject to actual configuration.
- **Information from other sources:** business contact information from public company websites, business directories, events, referrals or service providers only where the source and intended use have been assessed and the applicable legal requirements are met.

We do not intentionally seek sensitive personal data for prospect marketing. Do not submit sensitive personal information through general enquiry channels unless it is necessary for a specific service and we have explained the relevant safeguards.

## 3. Where does information come from?

We collect personal data:
- directly from you when you contact us, request a demo, create an account, subscribe, attend an event or provide business details;
- from your employer or an authorized representative where they provide your details;
- from public business sources or third parties where permitted by law and after assessing source reliability, relevance, terms and the intended use;
- automatically from our website, applications and security systems, subject to applicable cookie and tracking rules.

Where we obtain personal data indirectly, we record the source and collection date and provide the required privacy information within the applicable legal period. For Kenya, our controls account for the fourteen-day notice requirement in Regulation 6(3) of the Data Protection (General) Regulations, 2021. For UK GDPR, our controls account for Article 14 and the applicable earlier communication triggers and one-month outer limit. A notice does not itself create consent or authorize marketing where consent or another condition is required.

## 4. Why do we use personal data and what is the legal basis?

The legal basis depends on the purpose, the person's location, the data and the channel. We identify the applicable basis before processing and do not treat one basis as interchangeable with another.

| Purpose | Possible basis, subject to jurisdiction and facts |
|---|---|
| Respond to a request, provide a demo or take steps requested before a contract | Steps at the individual's request / contract where applicable; otherwise another lawful basis confirmed for the facts |
| Provide and administer subscribed services | Contract; legal obligation where applicable |
| Billing, accounting and legal compliance | Contract and/or legal obligation as applicable |
| Protect services, prevent fraud and secure accounts | Legitimate interests or another applicable basis, with necessity and balancing where required |
| Improve products and understand service use | Consent where required; otherwise a documented lawful basis and applicable cookie controls |
| Send service, security or transactional communications | Contract, legal obligation or the applicable relationship basis; marketing content is not disguised as transactional |
| Direct marketing | The jurisdiction-specific rules below; no marketing is sent merely because contact information is available |

### Kenya

VIORA's policy for prospect marketing is consent-first. We will only use personal data for direct marketing where the applicable requirements are met, including evidence that the data was collected from the data subject, that direct marketing was identified as a purpose, that the person consented to that use/disclosure, that a simplified opt-out is available and that no opt-out has been made. Public availability or a fourteen-day indirect-collection notice is not treated as consent or as curing a failure to meet the direct-marketing conditions. We will not send Kenyan prospect marketing where the source/consent evidence is incomplete.

### United Kingdom

For email marketing to a corporate subscriber under PECR, consent may not be required by the PECR email rule, but VIORA will still comply with UK GDPR when processing personal data about a named business contact. Where legitimate interests is relied upon, VIORA will document the purpose, necessity and balancing tests, assess relevance and reasonable expectations, provide privacy information and honor objections. Sole traders and certain partnerships are treated as individual subscribers; we will not email them for marketing without consent or a specifically confirmed lawful exception. If subscriber status is uncertain, we will apply the more protective classification and block the send unless the required evidence exists.

### United States

Commercial email is subject to CAN-SPAM requirements, including accurate sender information, non-deceptive subject lines, clear advertisement identification, a valid physical postal address and a working opt-out. We will not send US commercial email until the address used in the message has been documented and confirmed as meeting the statutory definition. B2B status does not remove CAN-SPAM obligations.

### Other countries

We do not assume that Kenya, UK or US rules authorize marketing in another country. We will not conduct prospect marketing in a jurisdiction until its applicable rules have been assessed and the relevant country/channel controls are approved.

## 5. How do we use data for business marketing?

We may research organisations to determine whether VIORA's services are relevant to their publicly stated business activities. We aim to use only limited business contact information and avoid sensitive or unrelated personal data.

Before any marketing communication, we assess:
- recipient jurisdiction and legal form/subscriber classification;
- source and collection method;
- required consent or other lawful basis;
- required privacy notice and evidence of delivery;
- message relevance and frequency;
- sender identity, opt-out and postal-address requirements;
- suppression and objection records; and
- human approval of the exact recipient and message.

We do not send marketing to people who have objected or unsubscribed. You can object to direct marketing at any time using the opt-out method in the message or by contacting support@viora.co.ke. We will apply suppression so that the address is not selected for future marketing. We may retain a minimal suppression record for the purpose of respecting your choice.
