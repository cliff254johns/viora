Data Processing Agreement

Last updated: 3 November 2026 · Effective: 3 November 2026

How to use this DPA: This Data Processing Agreement ("DPA") forms part of the agreement between VIORA Ecosystems (Processor) and the Client (Controller) for services involving the processing of personal data. It is governed by Kenyan law and includes optional international arbitration. To execute it, both parties sign a copy or reference it in the main service agreement.

This Data Processing Agreement ("DPA") forms part of the agreement between:

VIORA Ecosystems ("Processor" or "VIORA"), a business registered in Kenya, with its principal operations conducted online at viora.co.ke, contactable at support@viora.co.ke and +254 713 428 774;

and

The Client ("Controller"), the entity identified in the associated service agreement or order form.

1. Definitions

  • "Data Protection Law" means the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019 ("KDPA"), together with, where applicable, the EU General Data Protection Regulation ("GDPR"), the UK GDPR, and any other data protection laws applicable to the Processing.
  • "Personal Data", "Processing", "Data Subject", "Controller", "Processor" have the meanings given in the applicable Data Protection Law.
  • "Services" means the software, digital services, and support provided by VIORA under the main agreement.
  • "Sub-processor" means any processor engaged by VIORA to process Personal Data on behalf of the Controller.

2. Roles and scope

2.1 The Controller is the entity that determines the purposes and means of Processing Personal Data submitted to the Services.

2.2 VIORA acts as Processor and Processes Personal Data only on the Controller's documented instructions, as set out in this DPA and the main agreement.

2.3 Categories of Data Subjects may include: the Controller's customers, employees, contractors, and other individuals whose data the Controller submits.

2.4 Categories of Personal Data may include: identification data, contact data, transactional data, HR and payroll data, and other data the Controller submits through the Services.

2.5 Duration of Processing corresponds to the duration of the Services under the main agreement, plus any retention period required by law.

3. Processor obligations

VIORA shall:

  • (a) Process Personal Data only in accordance with the Controller's documented instructions, unless required by law (in which case VIORA will notify the Controller, unless legally prohibited);
  • (b) Ensure that personnel authorised to Process Personal Data are bound by confidentiality obligations;
  • (c) Implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect Personal Data, taking into account the state of the art, the costs of implementation, and the nature, scope, context and purposes of Processing;
  • (d) Assist the Controller in responding to Data Subject requests;
  • (e) Assist the Controller in ensuring compliance with Articles 32–36 of the GDPR and equivalent provisions of the KDPA, taking into account the nature of Processing and the information available to VIORA;
  • (f) Notify the Controller without undue delay after becoming aware of a Personal Data Breach, and provide reasonable assistance in the Controller's breach notification obligations;
  • (g) At the Controller's choice, delete or return all Personal Data at the end of the provision of Services, and delete existing copies unless storage is required by law;
  • (h) Make available all information reasonably necessary to demonstrate compliance with this DPA, and allow for and contribute to audits, including inspections, by the Controller or an auditor mandated by the Controller (subject to reasonable notice and confidentiality).

4. Sub-processors

4.1 The Controller grants VIORA general authorisation to engage sub-processors, subject to the conditions in this section.

4.2 VIORA currently engages the following sub-processors:

  • Cloudflare, Inc. — content delivery, security, and DNS. Data may be Processed in Cloudflare's global network.
  • Google Firebase (Google LLC) — hosting, authentication, database, and messaging. Data may be Processed in Google Cloud regions selected by VIORA.

4.3 VIORA shall impose data protection obligations on each sub-processor that are no less protective than those in this DPA.

4.4 VIORA shall notify the Controller of any intended changes to sub-processors, giving the Controller the opportunity to object.

5. International transfers

5.1 The Controller acknowledges that VIORA and its sub-processors operate internationally, and that Personal Data may be transferred to and Processed in countries outside the Controller's jurisdiction, including but not limited to the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, the United States, and other locations where Cloudflare and Google operate.

5.2 Where Personal Data originating in the EEA or UK is transferred outside those regions, VIORA shall implement appropriate safeguards, including the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) or equivalent mechanisms.

5.3 For Personal Data originating in Kenya, VIORA shall comply with the cross-border transfer requirements of the KDPA, including any registration or notification obligations.

6. Security measures

6.1 VIORA shall implement and maintain appropriate technical and organisational security measures, including:

  • Encryption of data in transit (TLS) and at rest (where supported by the underlying platform);
  • Access controls and authentication;
  • Logging and monitoring;
  • Regular backups;
  • Incident response procedures;
  • Secure development practices.

6.2 VIORA is currently working towards formal information security certifications. At the date of this DPA, VIORA does not claim ISO 27001 or SOC 2 certification. VIORA will update this DPA as such certifications are achieved.

7. Data subject rights

7.1 Taking into account the nature of the Processing, VIORA shall assist the Controller by appropriate technical and organisational measures in fulfilling the Controller's obligation to respond to requests to exercise Data Subject rights.

7.2 If VIORA receives a request directly from a Data Subject, it shall, where permitted, forward the request to the Controller and shall not respond directly unless instructed.

8. Liability and indemnity

8.1 Each party's liability under this DPA is subject to the limitations set out in the main agreement, except where Data Protection Law requires otherwise.

8.2 VIORA shall be liable for the acts and omissions of its sub-processors to the same extent it would be liable for its own acts and omissions.

9. Term and termination

9.1 This DPA remains in force for as long as VIORA Processes Personal Data on behalf of the Controller.

9.2 On termination, VIORA shall, at the Controller's choice, delete or return Personal Data, subject to any legal retention requirements.

10. Governing law and dispute resolution

10.1 This DPA is governed by the laws of Kenya.

10.2 Any dispute arising out of or in connection with this DPA shall first be referred to good-faith negotiations between the parties.

10.3 If the dispute is not resolved within thirty (30) days, it shall be finally resolved by arbitration under the Arbitration Rules of the Nairobi Centre for International Arbitration (NCIA), or, at the Controller's election where the Controller is established outside Kenya, by arbitration under the ICC Arbitration Rules with the seat of arbitration in Nairobi, Kenya. The language of arbitration shall be English.

10.4 Nothing in this section prevents either party from seeking urgent injunctive relief from a court of competent jurisdiction.

11. Order of precedence

In the event of conflict between this DPA and the main agreement, this DPA prevails with respect to the Processing of Personal Data.
