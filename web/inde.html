      Questions

      The things people actually ask

      Do I have to buy all nine portals?
      No. Start with the one that hurts most — usually Business UI or Finance. Every portal shares one data core, so adding Public, Partner, Training, Client, Product, YouTube, Presentation or Finance later takes days, not months.
      What exactly does the Finance portal do?
      Real-time P&L, cash-flow forecasting, multi-currency ledgers, automated bank and M-Pesa reconciliation, VAT/PAYE/NSSF/SHIF/Housing Levy computation, eTIMS-ready invoices, and board-ready reports. It reads from the same ledger every other portal writes to — so there's never a double entry.
      Will it work on my phone?
      Yes. Every portal is mobile-first and installable as a PWA on Android and iPhone. Your team can check in, log work, invoice and approve from the field — no laptop required.
      What about M-Pesa and KRA?
      M-Pesa Daraja integration handles STK push, paybill and till collections, then auto-reconciles each payment against the right invoice. Invoicing is eTIMS-ready, and payroll handles PAYE, NSSF, SHIF and the Housing Levy.
      Is my data safe?
      Encrypted in transit and at rest, role-based access, two-factor authentication, daily backups and a complete audit trail. Your data is yours — export it any time in CSV or PDF.
      How long does setup take?
      Business UI typically goes live in 2–4 weeks depending on how much data we migrate. Each additional portal — including Finance — is usually 1–2 weeks after that.
      Do you train my team?
      Always. Training happens on your own live data, in your own language and workflow — not on a demo account nobody recognises. With the Training UI, every certificate and completion is tracked automatically.
