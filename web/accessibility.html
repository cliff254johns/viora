Accessibility Statement

Last updated: 3 November 2026 · Effective: 3 November 2026

Our commitment: VIORA Ecosystems is committed to making our website and services accessible to everyone, including people with disabilities. We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 Level AA. As a young company (founded 2022), we are actively working towards full conformance and welcome your feedback.

1. Conformance status

Current status: Partially conformant

We are partially conformant with WCAG 2.2 Level AA. "Partially conformant" means that some parts of the content do not yet fully conform to the accessibility standard. We are actively working to close the remaining gaps and will update this statement as improvements are made.

2. Measures we take

  • Accessibility is considered from the design stage of new features and pages.
  • We test key user journeys with a combination of automated tools (axe-core, Lighthouse) and manual checks.
  • We aim to support keyboard navigation, screen reader compatibility, and visible focus indicators across the site.
  • We respect the prefers-reduced-motion setting for animated content.
  • We use semantic HTML and ARIA attributes where appropriate.

3. Known limitations

As a growing company, we are aware of the following limitations and are actively working to address them:

5. Compatibility

Our website is designed to be compatible with:

  • Browsers: Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge — current and previous major versions.
  • Assistive technology: NVDA and JAWS on Windows, VoiceOver on macOS and iOS, TalkBack on Android.
  • Operating systems: Windows, macOS, iOS, Android, Linux.

6. Technical specifications

Accessibility of our website relies on HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and WAI-ARIA (where needed).

7. Assessment approach

VIORA assesses accessibility through self-evaluation using automated tools, manual checks with keyboard and screen readers, and feedback from users. We do not currently carry a third-party accessibility certification.

8. Legal framework

We aim to align with:

  • WCAG 2.2 Level AA.
  • The EU Web Accessibility Directive and European Accessibility Act (where applicable).
  • The UN Convention on the Rights of Persons with Disabilities.
  • Kenyan disability rights legislation, where applicable.

9. Changes to this statement

We will update this statement as we make accessibility improvements. The "Last updated" date at the top reflects the most recent change.
