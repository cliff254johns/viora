What your client account gives you

A single place to follow the services VIORA has set up for your organisation. Your available sections depend on your engagement, assigned permissions and active subscriptions.

01 · Create

Register your account

Use your work email and submit the details requested. Registration starts an access request; it does not automatically attach you to an existing organisation or project.

02 · Connect

VIORA verifies your access

Your account is linked to the correct client organisation and the relevant team permissions. Some services or sections may require an active agreement, subscription or administrator approval.

03 · Manage

Use your workspace

Sign in to view the projects, invoices, documents, support requests, subscriptions and training resources that have been made available to your account.

What is included? Your client account provides a secure entry point and a consolidated view of VIORA services assigned to you. It does not itself purchase software, activate a paid subscription, guarantee project milestones, or grant access to another organisation’s information. Paid products, optional services, training enrolments and project deliverables are provided according to your agreement and account permissions.

The dashboard illustration above uses sample interface elements, not a real client account or live customer figures. Your portal displays information associated with your own verified account.

Real-time project tracking

Follow projects and milestones shared with your account, review current status and see the latest updates your VIORA team has published.

Invoices & M-Pesa payments

Review invoices, balances and recorded payments. Download available billing documents and use the payment options enabled for your account, including M-Pesa where configured.

Documents & presentations

Access documents, presentations and deliverables shared with your account. Uploads are available where the relevant client workflow has been enabled.

Direct support tickets

Raise and follow support requests, share the details the team needs and keep the conversation linked to your client workspace.

Training & onboarding

Open training and onboarding resources assigned or made available to your organisation. Course access and certificates depend on enrolment and completion requirements.

Subscriptions & service access

See the subscriptions and services associated with your account, check their status and follow renewal or access requests. Product access depends on an active entitlement.
