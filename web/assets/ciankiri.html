🐝 About Ciankiri Beekeeping SHG

Empowering the community through sustainable beekeeping in the heart of Tharaka Nithi County.

Rooted in Community, Nourished by Nature

Ciankiri Beekeeping Self Help Group is a community-based organization nestled in Ciakariga, Tharaka Nithi County. We are passionate beekeepers dedicated to producing the finest raw honey and natural bee products using traditional, sustainable methods.

Our bees forage on the pristine wildflowers of the Tharaka Nithi landscape, giving our honey its unique, rich flavor and exceptional quality. Every jar supports local families and promotes environmental conservation.
