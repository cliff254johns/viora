Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers regarding consultations, retainers, and legal processes.

How do I book an initial consultation?

You can fill out our online consultation booking form below, or call our office directly. Our team will verify availability and confirm an appointment slot within 24 hours.

What documents should I bring to my consultation?

Please bring a valid national ID or passport along with all relevant legal documents, contracts, correspondence, or official notices related to your legal inquiry.

Do you offer retainer services for ongoing business advice?

Yes, we offer tailored corporate retainer packages that provide continuous legal oversight, contract drafting, and statutory compliance assistance for businesses.

What are your consultation fees?

We offer a free initial 30-minute consultation for new clients. Subsequent consultations are billed at competitive hourly rates or fixed-fee packages depending on the complexity of the matter.
