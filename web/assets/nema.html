Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about SHERQ & ESG in Kenya

What is SHERQ and why does it matter for Kenyan businesses?
SHERQ stands for Safety, Health, Environment, Risk, and Quality. It's a holistic management approach that ensures compliance with Kenyan laws (OSHA, NEMA, Water Resources Authority) and improves operational efficiency, reduces costs, and enhances reputation.
Are your training programs accredited in Kenya?
Yes. All our SHE training is NITA-accredited and recognized by the Ministry of Labour. We also offer NOSA, IOSH, and ISO lead auditor certifications.
How can I nominate for the SHERQ Impact Awards Kenya?
Nominations open annually until August 31st. Visit our awards page or contact us directly to receive the nomination kit. Winners announced at the Nairobi Gala in November.
Do you offer on-site training across Kenya?
Absolutely. Our trainers travel to any county – from Nairobi to Kisumu, Mombasa to Eldoret. We customize training to your specific industry and workplace hazards.
What ESG frameworks do you follow?
We align with GRI, SASB, TCFD, and the UN Sustainable Development Goals, adapted to Kenya's unique regulatory environment (e.g., Climate Change Act, 2016).
How do I get started with a SHERQ audit?
Simply contact us for a free initial consultation. We'll conduct a preliminary gap analysis and provide a tailored proposal for your organization.
