About My Suits

Founded by Kimathi Mworia, we blend timeless craftsmanship with modern sensibility.

Our Story

My Suits · Stitch In Time was born from Kimathi's lifelong passion for classic tailoring and his mission to make high‑quality bespoke clothing accessible to everyone. With over 15 years in the fashion industry, Kimathi has built a brand that stands for excellence, personalization, and impeccable fit.

Every garment is crafted with the finest fabrics and an eye for detail that ensures you stand out with confidence. We believe a well‑tailored suit is more than clothing — it's a statement of character, ambition, and respect for the art of dressing well.

Mission & Vision

Mission: To empower individuals to express their unique style through perfectly fitting, sustainable, and timeless garments that elevate their personal and professional presence.

Vision: To become the most trusted bespoke tailoring house in Kenya, known for uncompromising quality and a customer‑centric approach.
