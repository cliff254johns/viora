06 — YOUR FIT

Get the right size

Fit should feel effortless.

Your best fit starts with a simple measurement. Measure both feet late in the day, use the longer foot, and compare the result with the size guide supplied by the retailer before placing an order.

  • Measure standing. Keep your heel against a wall and measure to your longest toe.
  • Check the width. Your toes should spread naturally without pressure at the sides.
  • Allow movement. Leave a little space in front without letting the heel slip.

07 — LONGER LIFE

Care, not clutter

Keep your favourites in rotation.

A little maintenance goes a long way. Remove surface dust after wear, clean gently with a soft brush and mild solution, and let your sneakers air-dry away from direct heat.

  • Brush before washing. Lift dry dirt first to avoid rubbing it deeper into the material.
  • Spot-clean gently. Test cleaners on a discreet area and follow the material-specific care instructions.
  • Dry naturally. Avoid radiators and harsh sunlight; use paper to help hold the shape.
