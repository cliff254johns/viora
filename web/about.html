Our story

Built by operators,
for operators.

VIORA started because we kept seeing the same problem: great businesses held back by scattered tools. A POS that doesn't talk to accounting. A payroll system that ignores attendance. A website that can't see inventory.

So we built an ecosystem instead — where every module shares the same data, the same users, the same security. When you add a VIORA system, it doesn't create work. It removes it.

Our mission

Make connected technology accessible to every business — not just enterprises.

Whether you run a shop, a clinic, a farm or a factory — you deserve systems that fit how you actually work. That's what VIORA exists to deliver.

Our values

What guides every decision.

Practical over impressive

We build systems that work on day one — not demos that look good in a pitch deck. Every module ships usable, tested and documented.

Connected by default

Every module shares data with every other. That's the whole point of an ecosystem — one source of truth, no more re-entering the same data.

Long-term partners

We measure success by how much value our clients still get years after deployment — not by how fast we closed the deal.

Our journey

From first client to full ecosystem.

2022

VIORA founded

Started in Nairobi with a mission to give SMEs access to the kind of integrated systems only big enterprises could afford.

2023

First 25 clients live

Retail POS, HR and payroll deployments across Nairobi and Mombasa. The ecosystem starts forming.

2024

Custom software practice

Launched the custom development arm — building portals, mobile apps, and API integrations tailored to specific industries.

2025

100+ projects, 35 industries

From Jua Kali workshops to hospitals, law firms to logistics — VIORA now serves businesses across every sector of the Kenyan economy.

Today

One integrated ecosystem

Business management, digital services, commerce, training and support — all connected through a single VIORA account.
