Cookie Policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026 · Effective: 3 November 2026

Summary: We use a small number of cookies and similar technologies. Essential cookies are always set because the site cannot function without them. 1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help the site remember your preferences, keep you signed in, and understand how the site is used. Similar technologies include local storage, session storage, and pixels.

2. Who we are

5. How to control cookies

5.1 Our cookie banner

When you first visit the site, a banner asks for your consent for non-essential cookies. You can change your choice at any time via the "Cookie settings" link in the footer.

5.2 Browser settings

Most browsers allow you to refuse or delete cookies. Instructions vary:

  • Google Chrome
  • Mozilla Firefox
  • Safari
  • Microsoft Edge

Blocking essential cookies may prevent parts of the site from working.

6. Do Not Track

We respect the Do Not Track (DNT) browser signal where technically feasible. When DNT is enabled, non-essential analytics will not be loaded.
