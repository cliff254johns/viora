Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026 · Effective: 3 November 2026

Plain-language summary: VIORA Ecosystems is an online-only business. We collect only what we need to provide our services, we store it with Cloudflare and Google Firebase, and we protect it with encryption in transit and at rest. You have rights over your data — see Section 8. Questions? Email support@viora.co.ke.

2. Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, share and protect personal data when you:

  • visit our website;
  • book a demo or contact us;
  • subscribe to or use our software and digital services;
  • interact with us on WhatsApp or by email.

Where we process personal data on behalf of a business customer (for example, their employees or their customers), we act as a processor and the customer is the controller. That relationship is governed by our Data Processing Agreement.

3. What personal data we collect

3.1 Information you give us

  • Contact details — name, email address, phone number, company name.
  • Business information — industry, required solutions, project description.
  • Account data — if you create an account: login credentials (stored hashed), role, preferences.
  • Communications — messages you send us by email, WhatsApp, or our contact form.
  • Payment data — billing details. Card data is handled by our payment processors; we do not store full card numbers.

3.2 Information collected automatically

  • Technical data — IP address, browser type and version, device type, operating system, referring URL.
  • Usage data — pages visited, time on page, features used, errors encountered.
  • Approximate location — derived from IP address (country/region level). We do not request precise geolocation unless you explicitly allow it.

3.3 Information from third parties

  • Authentication providers (if you sign in with a third-party account).
  • Payment processors (confirmation of payment status).

5. Who we share data with

We share personal data only with the sub-processors needed to run our services:

  • Cloudflare, Inc. — content delivery, security, DNS. Cloudflare operates a global network.
  • Google Firebase (Google LLC) — hosting, authentication, database, messaging. Data is stored in Google Cloud regions selected by us.
  • Payment processors (where applicable) — to process payments securely. Card data is handled entirely by the processor.
  • Email and communication providers — to deliver transactional email and support messages.
  • Professional advisers — lawyers, accountants, auditors, where necessary and under confidentiality.
  • Authorities — where required by law, court order, or to protect rights and safety.

We do not sell personal data.

6. International transfers

VIORA operates online and internationally. Personal data may be transferred to and processed in countries outside your own, including the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, the United States, and other locations where Cloudflare and Google operate.

Where personal data originating in the EEA or UK is transferred outside those regions, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses. For data originating in Kenya, we comply with the cross-border transfer requirements of the Kenya Data Protection Act 2019.

7. How long we keep data

  • Enquiry and demo data — up to 24 months after last contact, unless you become a customer.
  • Customer account data — for the duration of the subscription and up to 12 months after termination, unless longer retention is required by law.
  • Billing and tax records — for the period required by Kenyan tax law (currently 5 years).
  • Website logs — typically up to 90 days, unless needed for security investigations.
  • Marketing consent records — for as long as consent is active, plus a reasonable period to demonstrate compliance.

8. Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Delete your data, subject to legal retention obligations.
  • Restrict or object to certain processing.
  • Port your data to another provider in a machine-readable format.
  • Withdraw consent at any time where processing is based on consent.
  • Lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority — in Kenya, the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (ODPC); in the EU, your local data protection authority.

To exercise any right, email support@viora.co.ke. We will respond within 30 days (or the period required by applicable law).

9. Cookies and similar technologies

We use cookies and similar technologies as described in our Cookie Policy. Where required by law, we ask for your consent before setting non-essential cookies.

10. Security

We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect personal data, including:

  • Encryption in transit (TLS) and at rest where supported by the underlying platform.
  • Access controls and strong authentication.
  • Logging and monitoring.
  • Regular backups.
  • Incident response procedures.

No system is perfectly secure. If you believe your data has been compromised, contact us immediately at support@viora.co.ke.

11. Children

Our services are intended for businesses and are not directed at children under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top reflects the most recent change. Material changes will be communicated by email to registered users or by a prominent notice on our website.
